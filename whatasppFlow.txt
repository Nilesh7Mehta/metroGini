2A — Proactive re-engagement (AI-initiated, WhatsApp template message)
⚙ Logic note: Trigger: 24–48 hrs after app install with zero in-app activity (no booking, no cart).

Scenario 3 — Promotion: New Customer (Browsed / Cart / Chatted Before, No Booking)
Meta Ad → App Browsed / Added to Cart / Prior WhatsApp Chat (no completed booking) → Returns → AI Conversation → Booking
Context: Customer has shown intent — browsed the app, added a wash to cart, or previously chatted on WhatsApp — but dropped off before booking. When they say 'Hi' again, the AI should pick up the thread instead of starting from zero.

🙋 Customer:  Hi
🤖 Metrogini AI:  Welcome back to MetroGini (Wash by Kilo) 👋
Looks like you were checking out a Wash by Kilo booking earlier — 14 to 18 garments (Appx 5–7 kg).
Would you like to pick up where you left off?
1. Continue my booking
2. Start a new booking
3. Know more about MetroGini
⚙ Logic note: CRM/session data (cart contents, last-viewed garment tier, or last WhatsApp message) is fetched to personalise the greeting. If no specific cart item exists but only a prior chat/browse session, the AI instead says: 'Welcome back! Ready to book your first wash with us?'

If Customer selects 1 — Continue my booking
🤖 Metrogini AI:  Great! Confirming your selection:
14 to 18 garments (Appx 5–7 kg) : ₹1050 to ₹1470 (+GST)
🎁 Your 15% first-order discount is still valid.
Flow continues directly into address → pincode → pickup date → confirmation (same as Scenario 1), skipping the garment-count question since it's already known.

If Customer selects 2 — Start a new booking
Runs the full standard booking flow from Scenario 1 (garment count → address → pincode → date → confirmation).

 
Scenario 4 — Retention: One-Time Customer, No Repeat Order
(Notification, AI-initiated) Customer placed exactly one order in the past → No activity since → Win-back Nudge → AI Conversation → Booking
Context: Customer completed one full order but hasn't booked again after a defined inactivity window (e.g., 21–30 days). This is a proactive retention/win-back message, not a customer-initiated chat.

⚙ Logic note: Trigger: last_order_date > 30 days ago AND total_orders = 1. Personalise with first name where available.
🤖 Metrogini AI:  Hi Karthik 👋
It's been a while since your last wash with MetroGini — we miss you! 🧺
Here's 10% off your next order, valid for the next 3 days.
1. Book Now
2. View Offers
3. Not interested

If Customer selects 1 — Book Now
🤖 Metrogini AI:  Welcome back! Should we collect from your saved address?
📍 A-204, Lotus Residency, Andheri West, Mumbai – 400058
1. Yes, use this address
⚙ Logic note: Known customers skip straight to garment-count pricing, then straight to address confirmation (saved address) → pickup date → confirmation, cutting the flow roughly in half vs. a fresh lead.

If Customer selects 2 — View Offers
Opens the Offers & Referral content block (current promotions, referral program).

If Customer selects 3 — Not interested
🤖 Metrogini AI:  No problem! We're here whenever you need us. Reply HI anytime to book, or STOP to opt out of offers.
⚙ Logic note: Log 'declined win-back' — suppress promotional (not transactional) messages for 30 days.

Scenario 10 — Delayed Pickup / Delivery Inquiry
Customer has an active booking → Pickup or delivery is running late → Customer messages to check status
Context: Can happen at any point after booking confirmation — before pickup, or after 'out for delivery'. AI checks the live order record before replying.

If an active order is found
🙋 Customer:  Where is my pickup? It's already 12:30 and no one has come.
🤖 Metrogini AI:  Hi Karthik, sorry for the delay! 🙏
Your rider Suresh K. is on the way — slightly delayed due to traffic.
New expected arrival: 1:15 PM
1. Track live
2. Talk to an agent
⚙ Logic note: Delay reasons and revised ETA are pulled from the rider-tracking system, not guessed by the AI. If no updated ETA is available, respond with an apology + immediate escalation instead of a made-up time.

If delivery (not pickup) is delayed
🤖 Metrogini AI:  Hi Karthik, apologies for the wait! 🙏
Your order MG24571 is out for delivery and slightly behind schedule.
New expected delivery: today by 7:00 PM
1. Track live
2. Talk to an agent

If no active order is found for this number
🤖 Metrogini AI:  I don't see an active order linked to this number. Could you share your Booking ID so I can check for you?
⚙ Logic note: If customer can't provide a Booking ID, or the delay exceeds a defined threshold (e.g., 2+ hours past window), auto-escalate to a human agent rather than looping on tracking questions.
 
Scenario 11 — Damaged, Missing, or Wrong Garment Complaint
Delivery completed → Customer reports an issue with the order → AI triages → Escalates to human care team
Context: Post-delivery complaint. The AI's job here is to acknowledge, categorise, and hand off — never to promise compensation or negotiate refunds itself.

🙋 Customer:  One of my shirts is missing from the delivery
🤖 Metrogini AI:  I'm really sorry to hear that, Karthik. Let's get this sorted quickly.
What's the issue with your order MG24571?
1. Missing item
2. Damaged item
3. Wrong item received
4. Something else

After category is selected
🤖 Metrogini AI:  Got it. Could you share:
• A short description of the item
• A photo, if you have one (for damaged/wrong item)
🙋 Customer:  [Sends description / photo]
🤖 Metrogini AI:  Thank you — I've logged this as Complaint #CMP-3391 against Booking ID MG24571.
Our care team will personally review this and reach out within 24 hours.
⚙ Logic note: AI must NOT quote compensation amounts, refund percentages, or resolution timelines beyond the standard SLA. This always routes to a human agent/complaints queue with the booking ID, category, and any photos attached.
 
Scenario 12 — Payment Failed / Double Charged
Customer attempts payment → Payment fails or is charged twice → Customer reaches out → AI triages → Resolution or escalation

Payment failed but amount deducted
🙋 Customer:  My payment failed but the amount got deducted from my account
🤖 Metrogini AI:  Sorry for the trouble, Karthik! Let me check this for you.
Could you share your Booking ID or the payment reference/transaction ID?
🙋 Customer:  [Shares transaction ID]
🤖 Metrogini AI:  Thanks. Failed payments that show as deducted are usually auto-reversed by your bank within 5–7 business days.
If it isn't reflected by then, we'll escalate this immediately on your behalf.
1. Retry payment now
2. Talk to support
3. I'll wait for the auto-reversal

Charged twice for the same order
🙋 Customer:  I was charged twice for the same booking
🤖 Metrogini AI:  I'm sorry about that! This needs a closer look from our support team.
Could you share both transaction IDs or screenshots?
🙋 Customer:  [Shares screenshots]
🤖 Metrogini AI:  Thank you. I've raised this as Ticket #PAY-8820.
Our team will verify and refund any duplicate charge within 3–5 business days.
⚙ Logic note: Double-charge and unresolved failed-payment cases always escalate to a human/finance queue — the AI collects evidence (IDs/screenshots) but does not process refunds itself.
 
Scenario 13 — Negative Feedback Follow-Up
Delivery completed → Customer rates the order 1–2 stars → AI follow-up → Categorise issue → Escalate / make it right
Context: Triggered by the star-rating prompt sent at the end of Scenario 9's delivery-completed notification.

🙋 Customer:  ⭐⭐ (2 stars)
🤖 Metrogini AI:  We're sorry your experience wasn't great, Karthik 😞
Could you tell us what went wrong?
1. Quality of wash
2. Delay in pickup/delivery
3. Damaged or missing item
4. Rider behaviour
5. Something else

Routing based on selection
Damaged/missing item → routes into Scenario 11 (Complaint flow)
Delay → routes into Scenario 10 acknowledgement + logged for ops review
Quality/Rider behaviour/Other → logged and escalated to human care team
🤖 Metrogini AI:  Thank you for letting us know — this helps us improve.
Our team will personally reach out within 24 hours to make this right.
⚙ Logic note: The AI should never offer a discount/refund/rewash on its own authority in response to negative feedback; it acknowledges, categorises, and escalates. Any goodwill gesture is issued by a human agent.
 
Scenario 14 — Human Agent Handoff / General Complaint
Customer explicitly asks for a person, or the situation needs human judgement → AI hands off with full context
Context: Catch-all trigger — customer types 'agent', 'talk to someone', 'complaint', 'this is not resolved', or selects 'Talk to an agent' / 'Need help' from any other scenario.

🙋 Customer:  I want to talk to a real person
🤖 Metrogini AI:  Of course! Connecting you to a MetroGini care specialist now 🙌
Typical response time: 10–15 minutes (9 AM – 9 PM).
⚙ Logic note: The handoff carries forward customer name, booking ID (if any), and the last 2–3 messages of context, so the customer never has to repeat themselves to the human agent.

🤖 Metrogini AI:  While you wait, is there anything quick I can help with?
1. Track my order
2. View my booking details
3. No, I'll wait for an agent
 
Scenario 15 — Unrecognized Input / Fallback
Customer sends free text that doesn't match any menu option, keyword, or expected input → AI recovers gracefully

🙋 Customer:  can u wash my sofa cover also lol
🤖 Metrogini AI:  Sorry, I didn't quite get that 🤔
Here's what I can help you with:
1. Book for Wash by Kilo
2. Know more about MetroGini
3. Know about Existing Booking
4. Talk to an agent
⚙ Logic note: First fallback: re-show the relevant menu for wherever the customer is in the flow (not always the main menu — e.g., mid-booking it should re-show that step's options).

If it happens twice in a row
🤖 Metrogini AI:  I want to make sure you get the right help — let me connect you to a care specialist.
⚙ Logic note: Two consecutive unrecognized inputs auto-escalate to a human agent (Scenario 14) instead of looping the customer through menus repeatedly.
