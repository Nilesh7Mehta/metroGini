IMPORTANT BUSINESS NOTE
Master WhatsApp doc: NO advance payment at booking; pay full amount after weigh.
Current app APIs still support ADVANCE payment on booking.
For WhatsApp booking, confirm with product: either skip advance or keep it.
If skip advance → booking confirmation APIs may need a WhatsApp-specific path.
